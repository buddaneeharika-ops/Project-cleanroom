menu_book Reading Manual

How to read percentages

Each cell shows what fraction of the canonical total has been ingested into the database for that state.

95–100% — Fully covered, data complete
80–94% — Mostly done, minor gaps
50–79% — In progress, half done
11–49% — Early stage, backlog exists
0–10% — Not started / critical gap

Formula (Retro / Caste / Booth)

ACs in DB / Total ACs in Mapping × 100

Formula (Form 20)

Elections pushed to DB / Elections expected × 100
