Reading Manual

ECI Primary (Electoral Commission of India)

  • 1. Caste Data and Booth Data percentages are calculated based on the total number of Assembly Constituencies (ACs) and the number of ACs for which data is currently available.
  • 2. Percentages for Retro Data and Form 20 Data are computed using the election type, election year, and the count of actual ACs for which data is available.

Axillary Sources

  • 1. The percentages are computed based on the total number of districts listed in the LGD directory.
  • 2. * Note: Census Districts are greater in number compared to the LGD District counts.
