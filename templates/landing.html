Source reference

What each signal covers.

Use this reference to understand the source systems and fields behind the coverage figures below.

Available ECI data points

PRIMARY
  • Booth DataAC, booth and voter details; polling station, ward, pincode and address.
  • Caste DataAC name, caste, sub-caste, category and percentage.
  • Form 20AC and booth number, party, candidate, votes and booth rank.
  • Retro DataCandidate, party, votes, election type and election year.
  • Geospatial MappingsAC, booth and voter EPIC mapping with latitude and longitude where available.

Available data of other sources

AUXILIARY
  • EjalshaktiVillage, Lgd code, block, panchayat, population and household data.
  • JoshuaDistrict, population, language and religion demographics.
  • LGD DataLGD, PC and AC codes with district, block and panchayat mappings.
  • Muslim CensusDistrict, sub-district, population and Muslim percentage.
  • SECC DataHouseholds, income, marital status, population, caste and age.
  • NREGA DataJob cards, person-days, payments, work completion and assets.
  • Election LensAC, voters, turnout, candidate, party, votes, gender, caste and age.
  • Other sourcesIndiastat, Know Your School, Pincode and Municipal election datasets.
Reading manual
  1. Caste and Booth Data percentages use the total number of Assembly Constituencies and the number of ACs currently available.
  2. Retro Data and Form 20 percentages use election type, election year and the count of actual available ACs.
  3. Daman & Diu and Dadra & Nagar Haveli are handled separately in this analysis, although officially combined by the Government of India.
  4. Other-source percentages are calculated using the district count in the LGD directory; Census district counts may differ.
